Toileting

Worming & Parasite Prevention

6 items

Pups up to 6 months of age should be wormed once a month. Beyond 6 months & adult, worm every 3 months. I treat fleas/ticks if there are visible signs, otherwise I prefer to use natural repellants.

✂️

Grooming & Coat Care

9 items

Regular ear plucking and cleaning is the best way to prevent itching and infection.

First Aid

1 item

Highly unlikely you'll ever need this — but good to have in your first aid kit just in case your pup goes off their food. A tiny amount rubbed into their gums help to keep their blood sugars up to prevent hypoglycaemia. Always consult your vet and/or breeder if your pup is off their food.
